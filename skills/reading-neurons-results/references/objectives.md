# Objectives, AOI types and uploads

From the Neurons knowledge base. Objectives decide which benchmark an asset is compared with and which
metrics count towards its NIS, so a result only makes sense next to its objectives.

## Objectives

Three fields, set per asset. Neurons detects them automatically; people should check them, especially
for niche or multi-purpose campaigns, and pick the most specific category. "Any" gives a general
benchmark.

**Purpose**

- **Brand building**: awareness and recognition rather than immediate sales; storytelling that builds
  familiarity and trust.
- **Conversion**: turning awareness into action (purchase, sign-up, follow) with direct, urgent
  messaging.

**Industry** (category, then subcategory)

- **FMCG**: Food & Beverage; Personal care & Beauty; Household products; Fashion & Accessories.
- **Durable goods**: Automotive; Consumer electronics.
- **Entertainment Media**: Sports & Gaming.
- **Health**: Pharma.
- **Services**: Finance & Insurance; Telecommunication & Internet; Travel & Hospitality (includes
  restaurants and fast food).

**Use case**

- **Digital advertising**: Display ads; Social media ads (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok);
  video ads (YouTube, Dailymotion); CTV ads.
- **Traditional advertising**: Out of home (images), Print (images), TV (video).
- **Products**: Packaging (images).
- **Websites**: Brand websites; E-commerce websites; Hero banners (desktop and mobile, images).

Each level also has an "Any …" option that averages across it.

## AOI types

Setting an AOI's type turns on AOI-level benchmarks and makes it count towards the NIS.

- **Branding**: the main brand or logo. A brand name written in running text doesn't count. If several
  brands appear, only the main one.
- **Product**: the physical product. Non-physical products aren't benchmarked; not available for
  Services.
- **Headline**: the prominent, attention-grabbing text, usually one line.
- **CTA**: the text or button that prompts a response. Website navigation isn't a CTA.
- **Body Text**: other text. Disclaimers aren't benchmarked.

Video supports **Branding** only, as an automatically placed box.

When comparing an original with a variant, keep the same AOIs on both, and check that AOIs still sit on
the moved elements; a misplaced AOI changes the score.

## Upload requirements

- **Images**: jpg, jpeg, png, webp. 50–5000 px per side (scaled down above 3000 px). Up to 20 MB.
- **Video**: mp4, mkv, avi, mov, webm. 128–5000 px per side (scaled down above 1024 px). 5–121 fps
  (analysed at 24 fps). 1–500 MB. 1 second to 7 minutes.
- **Mixed media** (animated): gif, mp4, webm, avi, mov, mkv, with the video limits.
- **Documents**: pdf, exactly one page, up to 20 MB.
